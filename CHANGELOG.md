# Changelog

## 0.2.1 · November 2026

- The headline becomes "Every place is the future of another" (in Portuguese, "Todo local é o futuro de outro"). No change to the method itself.

## 0.2 · November 2026

- **New premise:** the solution to a city's problem most likely already exists, somewhere else or at another time.
  Search before inventing. "Every city is the future of another."
- **The five-step way of thinking:** abstract, search in space and time, compare conditions, adapt with the community,
  test and record.
- **New template: the solution card**, with six fields: need, principle, conditions, local form, adoption, and
  coordinates.
- **The cycle gains a search stage** in month 2. Month 3 becomes field and adaptation.
- **The registry and the report record the origin of each idea:** adapted from a solution card, or new.
- **New section: related methods** (TRIZ, A Pattern Language, participatory action research, design thinking, and
  the scientific method).
- New principle 3, "The solution probably already exists." The rules that don't change stay the same; their section
  is now §8.

## 0.1.1 · November 2026

- English becomes the reference language. The Portuguese text moves to [pt-BR/](pt-BR/README.md) as a translation.
- File names in English: METHOD, TRADEMARK, CONTRIBUTING, DERIVATIVES, CHANGELOG, templates/.
- No change to the method itself.

## 0.1 · November 2026

First public version, written before the first complete cycle.

- Principles, the three floors (listening, challenge, implementation), and the roles.
- The six-month cycle, month by month.
- The idea registry, with authorship, versions, and derivatives.
- The rules that don't change.
- The cycle report, with the outcome of every idea.
- Templates: the listening sheet, the challenge sheet, and the report.
- The CC BY-SA 4.0 license and the trademark policy.
