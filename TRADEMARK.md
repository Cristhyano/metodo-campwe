# The Campwe name

The method is free. The name is not.

The CC BY-SA 4.0 license of this repository **does not grant trademark rights**: the license says so itself, in
section 2(b)(2). The names **Campwe** and **Fase Beta** and the Campwe logos belong to Campwe.

## Allowed, without asking

- Saying your work is **"based on the Campwe method,"** with a link to this repository. That is what the license asks.
- Talking about Campwe and the method in texts, classes, articles, and news.
- Running an entire cycle under your own name, your city's, or your institution's.

## Only with Campwe

- Calling an event an **"official Campwe event"** or a challenge **"Fase Beta."**
- Using the Campwe logo.
- Implying that Campwe organizes, certifies, or endorses something it does not.

To use the name for an official event, open an issue in this repository.

## Why

So that people who take part in a cycle know who is accountable for it. Anyone can run their own, and only cycles
Campwe takes part in can call themselves official.
