## Script for one meeting (about 90 minutes)

1. **Opening, 10 min.** Who we are, what will happen with what is said, and when the neighborhood will hear back.
2. **What needs to change, 30 min.** Each person speaks in their own words. Nobody corrects anybody.
3. **Grouping, 20 min.** Put together on paper what keeps coming up.
4. **Choosing, 20 min.** Each person marks what weighs most on their life.
5. **Closing, 10 min.** Read out what was chosen and give the date of the next meeting.

**Care:** record only the names of people who agreed. Photos only with consent.
